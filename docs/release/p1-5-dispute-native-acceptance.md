# P1.5 dispute resolution and native PDF sharing
Staging only. Baseline 208b2ca35d330992d94225833f68c21694450b35, all four jobs passed. No change to monetary engine, canonical ledger, release rules or statutory rates. Proposal and decision are private, immutable and keyed to original employee/period/revision/item. Authenticated payroll/owner actors require payroll.prepare, timesheet.lock and current review grant; no employee self-review; different maker and checker. A correction-required outcome is a referral only, not a payroll adjustment. One proposal and decision per item; appeal/reopen and notification are not implemented. Employee status exposes approved explanation only, not draft notes or reviewer identity. Authorized dashboard reads original cases; demo is read-only. Legal review required for retention, dispute rights, reason minimization and employee access after departure.

Native PDF: SDK58 pinned expo-file-system 58.0.14 and expo-sharing 58.0.23, exact registry integrity in lock; native OS share sheet, private cache, random directory, fresh authenticated revision before export, scope check before sharing and cleanup on exit/next export. Recipient copies are outside app control; no assertion of file encryption after sharing, nor physical-device verification from JS exports. Native share availability and Android/iOS binary acceptance remain release gates. Sources: https://docs.expo.dev/versions/v58.0.0/sdk/sharing/ and https://docs.expo.dev/versions/v58.0.0/sdk/filesystem/. CPA/legal/security and independent frontend review required, including keyboard/VoiceOver/TalkBack, color contrast, padding, fonts, layout and responsiveness. No production approval.
