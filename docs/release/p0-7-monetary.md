## Scope and evidence
This intentionally narrow 2025 weekly ordinary covered taxable attendance-only candidate calculates monetary gross from canonical locked v4 exact seconds and historical rates, monthly salary hourly conversion only with a separately reviewed divisor record (annual paid days, eight-hour policy), contributions from separately reviewed monthly agency bases, and cumulative weekly withholding from contiguous immutable same-employer snapshots with zero opening. The three agency amounts are allocated once in the declared monthly allocation week. The posting function accepts only tenant/period/employee identifiers; it atomically persists monetary and balanced accrual journal records. Does not disburse, export, file or expose a public RPC.

Not supported: 2026 contribution schedule until independent confirmation, MWE, nonworked paid holidays, leave, guaranteed monthly salary, benefits/supplementary pay, wage floors and special categories, prior-employer monetary posting, withholding refund/annual settlement, corrections, arrears, partial months, irregular pay frequency. Attestation inputs are privileged-only fixtures, not a deployed maker-checker workflow. Actual payroll requires completeness of pay calendar, all earnings and agency bases, independent proof of opening history and final allocation, verified actors, actual legal classification, and CPA validation. No production use of this restricted candidate. Cumulative arithmetic follows RR 11-2018 §2.79(B)(5)(a), pp29–30; applicability and cent-rounding must be independently verified. Existing contribution helper limits PhilHealth to 2025; 2026 5% public advisory does not alone certify the full table. Do not expand rule dates without verified evidence.
